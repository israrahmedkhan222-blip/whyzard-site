Why the world works the way it does

Short videos about nature, science and the history where the two collide.

Whyzard is a YouTube channel that explains one natural process or story at a time: why lightning zigzags, how volcanoes shape the weather, why the ocean is salty. Each video opens with a "Why...?" question, explains the answer step by step, and names a real source.

About this website

This site describes the Whyzard publishing tool, a small private application that prepares the channel's videos and uploads them to the Whyzard YouTube channel. It exists so that the channel's owner can connect the tool to YouTube through Google's official sign-in.

What the tool does with a Google account
  • Uploads finished videos to the Whyzard channel.
  • Reads the view and like counts of the channel's own videos, to decide which topics to cover next.

It is used only by the channel's owner. It is not offered to the public, and it does not ask viewers or visitors to sign in. Details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
