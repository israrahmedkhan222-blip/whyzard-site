Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

1. Who we are

Whyzard is a YouTube channel run by one person (the Whyzard channel). The "App" in this policy means the Whyzard publishing tool, a private application that prepares the channel's videos and uploads them to YouTube. The App is used only by the channel's owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else is asked to sign in or create an account.

2. Google user data the App accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google's official sign-in screen, the App uses the owner's own Google account for two purposes only:

  • Upload videos to the Whyzard YouTube channel (YouTube upload permission).
  • Read statistics of the channel's own videos, such as titles, view counts and like counts (YouTube read-only permission).

The App does not access viewers' data, comments, subscriber lists, private videos, email, contacts, files or any other part of the Google account.

3. How the data is used

  • Uploading: to publish finished videos, with their title, description, tags and thumbnail, to the Whyzard channel.
  • Statistics: to see which of the channel's own videos performed best, so future topics can be chosen. The titles and view counts of the channel's own videos may be included in text prompts sent to Google's Gemini API to help pick topics. No information about individual viewers is included.

The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with or transferred to any third party, except as needed to provide these features or to comply with the law.

4. How the data is stored

  • The sign-in authorisation (a refresh token) is stored as an encrypted secret in the settings of the owner's private GitHub repository. It is not published anywhere.
  • A short log of the channel's own video titles and their view and like counts is kept in a file in the same private repository.
  • Nothing about visitors to this website is collected by the App. This website is a static page and does not use cookies or analytics of its own.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Viewers and children

The App does not collect personal information from people who watch the channel. Videos on the channel are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can remove the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored token by removing the secret from the repository settings. After either step, the App can no longer reach the Google account.

8. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects how data is used, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Whyzard YouTube channel.
